About FocusFlow

FocusFlow is a productivity app designed specifically for people with ADHD. It uses time-based motivation, gamification, and ADHD-friendly UI patterns to help users stay focused and accomplish their goals.

All your data (tasks, sessions, notes, achievements) is stored locally on your device. No account required - works completely offline.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2025

Data Collection: We do NOT collect any personal data. All information (tasks, sessions, notes, settings) stays only on your device using local storage.

Third-Party Services: We don't use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. The app only uses standard Flutter packages for local functionality.

Your Rights: Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage will delete all data. We have no access to your information.
