Privacy Policy

FocusFlow - ADHD-Friendly Focus & Productivity App

Effective Date: May 3, 2025

🎯 Your data stays on your device. We don't collect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 No Personal Data Collected by Us

FocusFlow-ADHD does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) to our servers. All data you create (tasks, sessions, notes, templates, achievements, settings) remains entirely on your device using local storage (Hive).

1.2 Local-Only Data

The app stores the following information only locally on your device:

  • Tasks - title, description, estimated/completed Pomodoros, priority, tags, due date, completion status
  • Sessions - type, duration, start/end time, energy rating, linked task
  • Templates - name, type, duration, energy label
  • Notes - content, category, timestamps
  • Settings - timer durations, theme preference, notification preferences

This data is never sent to us or any third party. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

1.3 No Account or Backend

FocusFlow-ADHD does not require you to create an account. There is no login system, no cloud sync, and no external database. All functionality works completely offline.

2. How We Use Your Information

Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not use your information for any purpose. The local data is used only to provide the app's core features (timers, task management, library, etc.) as you intend.

3. Third-Party Services

FocusFlow-ADHD does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any external service.

The app uses only the following Flutter packages which are open source and do not transmit data externally:

  • flutter_riverpod (state management)
  • hive / hive_flutter (local storage)
  • go_router (navigation)
  • flutter_animate (animations)
  • share_plus (sharing - only when you explicitly tap a share button)
  • file_picker (exporting - only when you choose a file location)

Some device permissions may be requested (e.g., notifications), but these are used solely for local reminders and timers; no data is transmitted.

4. Children's Privacy

FocusFlow-ADHD is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because all data remains on the device and no personal information is collected, there is no risk of child data being transmitted.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored locally using Hive, an encrypted binary database (if you enable encryption). However, no security measure is 100% guaranteed. We recommend using device screen locks and keeping your operating system updated. FocusFlow-ADHD does not have access to your data; it remains under your sole control.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, correct, delete, or port. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Effective Date". We encourage you to review it periodically. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
